What We Do

Complete Kitchen Renovation Services

Every detail matters. We handle every aspect of your kitchen remodel — from initial design to the final walkthrough.

Custom Kitchen Design

We work with you to design a layout that maximizes function and beauty. From open-concept plans to galley kitchens, every design is tailored to your family's lifestyle and the architectural character of your Avon home.

Cabinet Installation & Refacing

Whether you need brand-new custom cabinetry or want to refresh your existing cabinets with modern doors and hardware, we deliver stunning results. Soft-close hinges, dovetail joints, and premium finishes come standard.

Countertops & Backsplashes

Granite, quartz, marble, and butcher block — we source and install premium countertops with precision seaming. Pair it with a hand-laid tile backsplash that ties the whole room together.

Plumbing & Fixtures

New sink installations, faucet upgrades, dishwasher hookups, and gas line work for ranges. We coordinate all plumbing so everything is up to code and functioning flawlessly on day one.

Electrical & Lighting

Under-cabinet LEDs, pendant lights, recessed cans, and updated outlets and circuits. We'll light up your kitchen beautifully while ensuring your electrical system meets modern safety standards.

Flooring & Finishing

Hardwood, luxury vinyl plank, tile — we install durable kitchen flooring that withstands daily life while looking incredible. Complete with trim, transitions, and paint to finish the space perfectly.
