What We Build & Renovate

From a single room refresh to a full interior renovation — we handle it all with clean design, quality materials, and craftsmanship you'll notice every day.

Most Popular

Kitchen Remodels

Full gut renovations or targeted refreshes — new layouts, custom cabinetry, countertops, backsplashes, and lighting designed around how your family actually cooks and lives.

Most Popular

Bathroom Remodels

Tile showers, soaking tubs, vanities, flooring, and fixtures — we transform dated bathrooms into clean, functional spaces that feel like they belong in a newer home.

Interior Renovations

Open a wall, reconfigure a layout, or update the feel of an entire floor — we help homeowners rethink spaces and then build them out with care.

Flooring Installation

Hardwood, luxury vinyl plank, tile, and laminate — we install beautiful, durable floors throughout your home, including proper subfloor prep and transitions.

Trim & Finish Carpentry

Crown molding, baseboard, casing, built-ins, and accent walls — the finish details that elevate a renovation from "nice" to "wow."

Tile & Backsplashes

Subway tile, mosaic, large-format stone — we hand-lay tile in kitchens, baths, mudrooms, and entryways with precise grout lines and a clean finished edge.

Decks & Outdoor Living

New deck builds, deck replacements, and outdoor spaces designed to extend the livable square footage of your home and hold up to Indiana seasons.

Drywall, Paint & Repair

Patch, texture, hang, and paint — from a single damaged wall to prepping an entire room for renovation, we handle the work that holds everything else together.

Handyman & Punch-List

Smaller jobs, fix-up lists, and project wrap-ups — if your home has a list of things that need attention, we're the team to knock it out efficiently and correctly.

Design & Planning Help

Not sure where to start? We'll walk through your space, talk through options and layouts, and help you build a clear plan before any money is spent on materials or labor.
